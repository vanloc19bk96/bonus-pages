The product

A Mystery Book Where the Picture Names the Killer

NAZO BOX makes murder mystery nonogram books. Readers fill in a grid of numbered squares, a picture appears, and that picture is the clue that solves the case.

Building these by hand is brutal: every picture needs a story, suspects, clues and a puzzle with exactly one solution. One mistake and you get a "this puzzle is broken" review. NAZO BOX's engine draws the picture, writes the clues, then solves its own puzzle to prove it. If it can't, the page never reaches you.

  1. CreatePick a difficulty, page size and layout. Click Build. Every puzzle is made and verified for you.
  2. EditChange any title, story line or picture. Reroll a case, change its difficulty, add pages anywhere.
  3. ExportDownload a KDP-ready interior PDF, plus PNG and JPG pages for Etsy or social posts.
